brand

One system, six accents.

Every d7r open source site shares the same design language: terminal ink, glass surfaces, mono brand moments, soft glows, restrained motion. Each specification owns exactly one degree of freedom, its accent. Emerald is reserved for the root.

  • The bright variant is a dark-surface color: chips, terminal cards, dark-theme accents.
  • The deep variant is the light-surface color, chosen for 4.5:1 contrast on white.
  • A site adopts its scheme with <html data-accent="saga">. Everything else follows.
